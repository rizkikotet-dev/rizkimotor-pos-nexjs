# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Admin and cashier staff at a physical motorcycle spare parts shop (Rizki Motor). The cashier needs fast, accurate checkout with stock visibility. The admin needs inventory oversight, transaction history, and store configuration. Both users are non-technical, working behind a counter during busy hours.

## Product Purpose

A point-of-sale and inventory management system for motorcycle spare parts. Combines a cashier POS for fast checkout, an admin dashboard for inventory and store management, and a public product catalog. Success means: checkout under 30 seconds, zero stock-count errors, instant transaction lookups.

## Brand Personality

Professional, Fast, Reliable. The app should feel like a well-maintained tool — clean, efficient, no surprises. Confident without being loud. Functional without being cold.

## Anti-references

Busy enterprise POS systems: cluttered dashboards with dozens of badges, competing cards, and too many colors on screen. The app should never feel overwhelming or demand interpretation. No SaaS marketing fluff inside operational screens. No legacy desktop aesthetics (gray bevels, tiny fonts, Windows-era chrome).

## Design Principles

1. **Speed-first interactions**: Every screen optimized for the fastest path to the user's goal. Cart-first POS, one-click actions, minimal navigation depth.
2. **Stock visibility**: Product availability is always visible, never hidden behind clicks. Color and position communicate status at a glance.
3. **Operational clarity**: No decorative elements in functional screens. Every pixel serves a purpose — identify, locate, act.
4. **Consistent across roles**: Admin and cashier surfaces share the same visual language. A cashier who becomes an admin sees the same app, not a different one.
5. **Print-ready outputs**: Receipts and barcodes are first-class citizens, not afterthoughts. Thermal printer output must be pixel-perfect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA basics: skip links, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, sufficient contrast. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Screen reader text for dynamic content (cart totals, stock changes). The primary users are behind a counter during business hours; the app must work reliably under fluorescent lighting and with basic accessibility needs.
